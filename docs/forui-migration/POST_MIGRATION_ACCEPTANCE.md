# Post-migration acceptance

This is the closing stabilization, regression and acceptance cycle of the
ForUI migration of MemoX v8, held on 2026-10-18 and targeting Android. iOS is
out of scope. It added no new design and no features. It fixed the defects
it found at their root and recorded what it could not verify.

Companions:
- [SCREEN_STATE_COVERAGE.md](SCREEN_STATE_COVERAGE.md): every screen and
  state, with its evidence.
- [REGRESSION_FINDINGS.md](REGRESSION_FINDINGS.md): every defect fixed or
  open in this cycle.

## 1. Final status: BLOCKED (environment), with every runnable gate passing

No critical regression is known, and every gate that could run in this environment passes. One mandatory gate, the Android build, could not run: the cloud environment's network policy blocks Google Maven. The device-level checks (`integration_test/`, TalkBack) and the real-stack auth tests could not run either. Under the task's definitions, the status is therefore **BLOCKED**, not ACCEPTED or CONDITIONAL. The blocker is the environment, not the code, and the table below states exactly what remains.
