## Priority issues
- [P1] Spec §10 misreads kit 09. The editor has a "More" card with "Move this card to Trash" and an outline Move to Trash button (FlashcardEditScreen.jsx 203–212), wired to delConfirm. §10 says the kit draws no control; the owner ruled on that premise. Fix: re-rule — build it in plan 1 (one-card flow, Undo, back to the list) or keep it out as a recorded 09 deviation. /impeccable harden
- [P1] Undo vanishes under TalkBack. MxSnackbar draws its action as its own TextButton, so SnackBar.action is null and `persist` defaults false (Flutter 3.47 scaffold.dart 612–628): the 8s Undo times out for a TalkBack user (WCAG 2.2.1). Fix: an Undo snackbar persists while `MediaQuery.accessibleNavigation` is on, and showMxSnackbar replaces the current snackbar so a persisting one never blocks the queue. /impeccable audit
- [P2] Blocked-purge note is a single banner at the list's foot, in the same family as the policy note; with two blocked batches it is unclear whether it repeats. Fix: one sentence per blocked batch naming both entries (D6 copy), warning tone, cleared when the list next changes. /impeccable clarify
- [P3] TalkBack and truncation: a row's origin and meta ellipsize; its semantics must carry the full path, kind, age and time left in one node. /impeccable audit
- [P3] No busy state on confirms: Move to Trash, Restore and Delete N must spin (MxSheetActions.isConfirmLoading) and refuse a second tap. /impeccable harden
