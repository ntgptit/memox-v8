## Design specificity
The screen is specific to MemoX:
- the card-day unit, "a card counts once per day" (BR-PROGRESS-011);
- a streak held from yesterday before today's first card (BR-PROGRESS-016);
- "resets change nothing here" (BR-PROGRESS-017);
- a deck's numbers cover its whole subtree, and the drill-down follows the tree.

## Priority issues

- **[P1] The range tray reads as if it controlled Today and Streak.**
  - *Problem:* at the library level the tray is the first control, above Today and Streak. Switching to "Last 30 days" changes only the list at the bottom, often below the fold. The person sees nothing change and doubts the tap. UC-PROGRESS-001 step 4 and UC-PROGRESS-002 step 1 put the range below the overview blocks, above the list.
  - *Fix:* at the library level, the tray sits directly above the list (after Streak). A deck's level has no overview, so its tray stays at the top. This is recorded as a deviation from the kit (UC beats the kit).
  - *Command:* `/impeccable layout`
- **[P2] Dimmed rows fall under contrast.**
  - *Problem:* the kit dims an inactive deck row to 60% opacity. Its 12 sp sub-line in `onSurfaceVariant` then falls below 4.5:1 on the card, in both themes.
  - *Fix:* keep the name and "No activity in this range" at full contrast in `onSurfaceVariant`, and show the figure 0 in `onSurfaceVariant` instead of `onSurface`. Nothing is dimmed by opacity.
  - *Command:* `/impeccable audit`
- **[P3] The header's trailing total repeats the total row.**
  - *Problem:* with D2's total row, "{n} active cards · {m} card-days" appears twice. At 360 dp in "Last 30 days" the header already wraps to two lines in the kit's own capture.
  - *Fix:* drop the header's trailing text; the total row carries the numbers.
  - *Command:* `/impeccable distill`
- **[P3] A bar's value is invisible to sight.**
  - *Problem:* the chart shows shape only, and the kit's value is a web tooltip.
  - *Fix:* keep the kit's bars (the numbers of today are above them). Each bar carries its TalkBack label (spec §7). No tooltip on Android.
  - *Command:* none; recorded.
- **[P3] Semantics.**
  - The tray is a single choice: each option is selected or not.
  - A deck row is one button node: "{name}, {n} cards, {d} active days, {l} learning, {r} reviewing".
  - The total row is one node and not a button.
